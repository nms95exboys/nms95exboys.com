Privacy policy: NMS95 Ex-Boys app

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what the NMS95 Ex-Boys app collects, why, and what you can do about it. The app is run by the NMS 95/2001 Ex-Boys Association ("the association", "we") for its invited members.

Contact: nms95exboys@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Your profile: name, NMS number, house, city and country, and, if you add them, phone number, profile photo and skills.
  • Your email address: used to send you sign-in codes.
  • What you post: photos, announcements, events and RSVPs, tributes, ideas and comments, welfare and mentoring requests, business adverts, visit check-ins and reports.
  • Money records: dues, contribution, welfare and fundraising payments you record, the amounts, dates and proof of payment you upload, and committee approvals. You pay the association by bank transfer outside the app. The app never takes card or bank payments and doesn't store your card details.
  • Device information: a push notification token, so we can send you notices you haven't muted.

We do not use advertising or analytics tools, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and the app never reads your phone's GPS location, contacts or microphone.

Who can see it

  • Approved members see the members directory (name, house, city, country, photo, and phone if you added it) and what members post.
  • Committee members see what their role needs: for example, Finance sees payment records and Welfare sees welfare requests. Welfare requests are not shown to other members.
  • Nobody outside the association. We never sell or share your information for marketing.

Service providers

We use these companies to run the app. They handle data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in), hosted in the EU (Ireland)
  • Resend (sends sign-in code emails)
  • Expo, Apple and Google (deliver push notifications)

How long we keep it

Your profile and posts are kept while you are a member. Money records are kept for as long as the association needs them for its accounts, even after you leave, because other members rely on them.

Deleting your account

Open Me → Delete my account in the app. This deletes your sign-in, profile, photo, phone number and the content you posted. Money records stay in the association's accounts under "Former member", without your contact details. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Your rights

You can see and correct your profile in the app at any time. To ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion, or to complain, email nms95exboys@gmail.com. If you are in the UK or EU you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Children

The app is for adult members of the association only.

Changes

We will post any change on this page and update the date above.
