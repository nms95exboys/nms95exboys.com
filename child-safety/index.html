NMS95 Ex-Boys app: child safety standards

The NMS95 Ex-Boys app is the private app of the NMS 95/2001 Ex-Boys Association. It is for adult members only: every member joins with an invite code from the association and is approved by the membership committee. The app is not meant for children.

Zero tolerance

We do not allow child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) or child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind in the app: no images, videos, text, links or requests that sexualise, endanger or exploit children.

How to report a concern

  • In the app: open the photo, idea, advert or mentoring post and tap Report. Moderators review every report.
  • Block: open the member's profile and tap Block member.
  • By email: write to nms95exboys@gmail.com with "Child safety" in the subject.

What we do

  • Remove the content as soon as we know about it.
  • Suspend the member's account and remove them from the association's app.
  • Report it to the relevant authorities, including the US National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), and to law enforcement where the member or the child is located.
  • Keep the information the authorities need, and nothing else.

Contact

Child safety point of contact: nms95exboys@gmail.com.
