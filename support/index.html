NMS95 Ex-Boys app: support

The NMS95 Ex-Boys app is the private app of the NMS 95/2001 Ex-Boys Association.

Need help? Email nms95exboys@gmail.com. Tell us your name, NMS number and what went wrong.

How do I join?

You need an invite code from the association. Install the app, enter your email, type in the sign-in code we email you, then enter your invite code (8 letters and numbers, like K7QM-3XPD). The membership committee then approves you.

I didn't get the sign-in code

Check your junk or spam folder for an email from codes@mail.nms95exboys.com, mark it "Not junk", and try again. Codes last one hour.

How do I report something?

Open the photo, idea, advert or mentoring post and tap Report. Moderators review every report.

How do I block a member?

Open their profile and tap Block member.

How do I delete my account?

Me → Delete my account. See the privacy policy for what is kept.
